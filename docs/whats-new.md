# Content desk: what's new (October 2026)

Built from the club president's feedback. Everything below is live at desk.zadok.dev.

## 1. Sizes for campus screens

Every template now downloads in three more sizes, next to the Instagram and LinkedIn ones:

| Screen | Size |
| --- | --- |
| WSU TV | 1280 × 720 |
| Woolsey Big TV | 1920 × 1080 |
| Shockerblast | 800 × 600 |

Pick the size above the preview (they're grouped under **Social** and **Screens**). The graphic
re-lays itself out for the wide screen: words on the left, photos, speakers and recap numbers on the
right. Nobody has to redesign anything. **All formats** downloads every size at once, and file
names say where each one goes (for example `...-wsu-tv-1280x720.png`).

## 2. Categories

Every post has a category: **Club events, News, Recaps, Highlights** or **Other**.

- New posts get one automatically from the series you pick; change it under **Look** in the editor.
- The post list has a second row of filters with a count per category.
- The **New post** window is grouped by category.

## 3. Uploading finished graphics

For anything made outside the desk (Canva, Figma, a sponsor's file), use **Upload a graphic** at
the top of the page. It becomes a post like any other: it gets a category, captions, review and
approval, and it shows in the shared list.

- The file is kept exactly as uploaded (up to 10 MB). **Download original file** gives it back byte
  for byte.
- It can also be downloaded in every format. **Fill the frame** crops the edges to fit (drag to
  choose what stays); **Show the whole graphic** fits it on an ivory or black background.
- The desk tells you which formats already match your graphic's shape, and warns you when a format
  will crop it.

Note: the old **Import** button only ever loaded `.json` backups (which is why it wouldn't take
pictures). It's now labelled **Import backup**, next to **Export backup**.

## 4. Adding new templates

**New post > Add a template** turns a background you designed elsewhere into a reusable template
that every officer sees.

- Upload a background for the feed at least, and optionally one per size (WSU TV, Big TV, etc.).
  A size without its own background borrows the main one, cropped to fit, and the desk says so.
- Choose ivory or dark text, where the words sit (bottom, middle, top, or no text), the club logo
  header, a shade behind the words, and a data strip for date, time and room.
- Saved templates appear in the **New post** window under their category, with an **Edit** button.
- Posts copy the template when they're made, so editing or deleting a template later never changes
  posts that already exist.

## Unchanged

Existing posts, photos, the review and approval steps, the brand checks, and live sharing between
officers all work as before. Old posts were given a category from their series.

## Good to know

- No login yet: anyone with the link can use the desk, so only share it with officers.
- The desk can't read words inside an uploaded graphic, so it can't check those for brand voice.
  Proofread uploads before approving them.
